BIO

"Like a twisted, feminine Little Nemo for our current generation, Damascan Daydreams transports us across a collective subconscious of exotic, electronic Pop-inspired sounds." -Jack Anderson, KUTX Austin

photo by Candice Ghai Photography

Damascan Daydreams is the Austin-based electronic dream-pop project of singer/songwriter Eman Tiba, blending washed-out synths, hypnotic rhythms, and emotionally charged vocals into immersive, dream-forward soundscapes.

The live band now includes members Joseph Salazar (Halo Infinite composer), Mike Hidalgo (Obsolete Machines), and Maddy Brotherton (The Unlimited Stars). Live performances emphasize atmosphere and emotional flow, pairing layered electronics with expressive vocals to create a sense of motion between the intimate and the cosmic. The project’s recent releases explore themes of connection, memory, and internal landscapes, drawing listeners into lush, genre-blurring environments that feel both personal and expansive.
